Privacy Policy

QuickSend for WhatsApp  ·  Effective: May 23, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 23, 2026

Introduction

QuickSend for WhatsApp ("the app") lets you send a WhatsApp message to any phone number without saving that number as a contact. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used. The short version: all data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Data We Collect

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Phone numbers you type into the app (kept in message history)
  • Message text you type (kept in message history)
  • Your preferences — chosen country code, selected WhatsApp variant (regular or Business), and display theme (light/dark)
This data never leaves your device. It is stored in Android's SharedPreferences storage, which is private to the app and not accessible to other apps.

How Data Is Used

The data is used solely to:

  • Pre-fill the phone and message fields from your history, saving you time
  • Remember your preferences between sessions

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no user profiling of any kind.

What Happens When You Tap Send

When you tap the send button, the phone number and message are passed directly to WhatsApp via an Android system Intent. From that point, WhatsApp handles the data according to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy. We have no visibility into or control over what WhatsApp does with that data.

Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored locally in Android SharedPreferences (plaintext, no encryption).
  • Google Cloud Backup is disabled — your history and preferences are never backed up to Google's servers.
  • Data is only accessible by this app on your device.

Third-Party Libraries

The app uses the following open-source libraries. None of them collect, transmit, or store any user data:

  • CountryCodePicker (hbb20) — displays the country code selector widget
  • Gson (Google) — serializes history data to local storage
  • CircleImageView — renders circular image views in the UI

Firebase Cloud Messaging was previously included in the app but has been fully removed. No analytics, crash reporting, or push notification service is active.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored message history at any time directly inside the app: tap the menu icon (⋮) → Clear History. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used this app and you have concerns, contact us at the address below.
