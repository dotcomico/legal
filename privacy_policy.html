Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Short version: To spot Reels, ScrollShift reads the text on your screen while Instagram is open. It checks that text on your phone and forgets it right away. Nothing is saved or sent. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends any data to us or to anyone else.

1. Who We Are

ScrollShift is an Android app that helps you avoid Instagram Reels and endless "suggested" feeds. When it detects one, it takes you back. If you choose, it can also show a short focus card or open another app you picked.

The app is developed by Dotan Siman Tov, an independent developer. Contact: dotan.simantov.dev@gmail.com

2. What the App Reads on Your Device

Screen text inside Instagram (Accessibility Service). The app uses Android's Accessibility Service and only receives events from the Instagram app. When Instagram's screen changes or scrolls, the app reads the text shown on that screen and looks for labels such as "Reels", "Explore" or "Suggestions for you". The screen can include other things, such as usernames, captions, comments or messages, and the app reads them along with everything else. However:

  • The text is checked in memory, on your phone, and discarded right away.
  • It is never saved, logged, sent to us, or shared with anyone.
  • The app does nothing in apps other than Instagram.
  • The service is used only to detect Reels and suggested feeds and to take the action you chose (go back, show the focus card, or open another app). It is never used for anything else.

Your list of installed apps. To let you choose which app to open after a block, the app shows the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon (name and icon). It also checks whether a short, fixed list of learning and wellbeing apps is installed, so it can suggest them. This happens on your phone only. The app saves only the apps you pick, and the list is never sent anywhere.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone number, or any device identifier.

3. What the App Stores on Your Device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • How many times Reels and suggested feeds were blocked
  • A log of recent blocks: the type ("Reels" or "feed") and the time, without any content. Only the latest 500 entries are kept. They power the weekly chart.
  • Your streak: the number of consecutive days with at least one block, and the date of the last block
  • The apps you chose to open after a block (stored as app package names)
  • Your settings, such as which blockers are on and what happens when one fires

Nothing on this list includes anything you saw or typed inside Instagram.

4. What Leaves Your Device

Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. The app does not request the internet permission and has no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code. We never sell or share any data.

Android backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the settings and statistics listed in section 3 in your own Google Account backup, and restore them on a new phone. Google runs this backup under its own terms, and you control it in your phone's settings. We cannot see or access it. Screen text is never part of it, because the app never saves screen text.

Opening another app. If you choose to open another app after a block, the app simply launches it and sends it no data. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

5. Focus Card, Sounds and Voice

The optional focus card can play a tone and use your phone's text-to-speech engine to read out fixed workout phrases, such as "Halfway there". It reads only these built-in phrases and numbers, never anything about you or from Instagram. Speech is produced by your device's text-to-speech service, which your phone provides and which follows its own privacy terms. While a sound plays, the app briefly pauses other audio (such as Instagram). The app never uses the microphone.

6. Permissions Used

  • Accessibility Service: reads screen text inside Instagram only, as described in section 2. You turn it on yourself after an explanation inside the app, and you can turn it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.
  • Display over other apps: shows the optional focus card after a Reels block. It's only used if you choose the focus card.
  • Vibrate: a short vibration when the focus card appears.
  • App visibility: lists apps with a launcher icon so you can choose one to open after a block, as described in section 2.

7. How Long Data Is Kept, and How to Delete It

Everything stays on your phone until you remove it. The block log keeps only its latest 500 entries. To delete all app data, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → ScrollShift → Storage → Clear storage. You can delete Android backups from your Google Account settings. The app has no accounts, and we hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

8. Security

The app's data lives in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent over the internet, nothing can leak in transit.

9. Your Rights

This policy applies to every user, in every country. Privacy laws such as the EU's GDPR and Israel's Privacy Protection Law give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We don't receive or hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You stay in full control of the data on your phone, as described in section 7.
